Build from source

Developers can use Xcode and Tuist on a Mac. Follow the development guide to generate the workspace, then open apps/ios/Yorozu.xcworkspace in Xcode. For a physical iPhone, configure your own signing team and provisioning for the app and its extensions, select the YorozuIOS scheme and your connected iPhone, then run.

Installed both apps? Pair once.

You’ll need Yorozu on your iPhone and an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 15 or later and OpenClaw configured. Keep your host Mac awake and online to reach it from your phone.

  1. 1

    Set up your host Mac

    Open Yorozu from the menu bar, choose “Host Yorozu on this Mac” and grant the permissions you want.

  2. 2

    Scan one code

    In Mac Settings → Devices, choose “Pair Another Device”. Open Yorozu on your iPhone and scan the code. No Yorozu account needed.

  3. 3

    Talk from anywhere

    Every thread lives on the Mac. Your phone, and any other Mac, just reaches it.

A yes or no, from wherever you are.

When an agent wants to run a command, send a message or spend money, the question lands on your phone as a card. Quick decisions get Allow and Don't allow right on the notification. Anything bigger opens the card, so nothing dangerous is one accidental tap away.

Say Discuss first and the agent explains before it acts.

Your Mac does the work.

OpenClaw answers by default, with the providers, models, tools and memory you configured there. Open a thread as Claude Code or Codex instead and it runs in a folder under ~/Projects, with that agent's own permissions.

Threads sync between the Mac and every paired device. Pick up on the desktop exactly where the phone left off.

Also supports iPhone Duo.

Open your iPhone Duo to see your sessions and conversation side by side.

The relay can't read a word of it.

Keys never leave your devices
An X25519 exchange at pairing time. The relay stores public keys and a room id, nothing it could open.
No Yorozu account
No email, no password, no analytics. Your provider logins stay in OpenClaw, Claude Code and Codex on the Mac.
Run it yourself if you like
The relay is a few hundred lines. Put it on a Mac mini over Tailscale and change one URL in Settings.

Before you install

Using Yorozu could get your Claude or ChatGPT/Codex account suspended or banned. Yorozu drives Claude Code and Codex on your Mac with the account you signed in with. Anthropic and OpenAI decide what use of their subscriptions is allowed, change those rules without notice, and have suspended accounts used with third-party agent tools. If you want the lowest risk, use an API key rather than a subscription login.

AI agents take real actions on your Mac: they run commands, edit and delete files, and can send messages. Review what you approve. See the Terms of Use.
