Your Mac's agents.
In your pocket.

Yorozu (よろず) connects your iPhone to OpenClaw, Claude Code and Codex running on your own Mac. Start a task, answer an approval from the lock screen, pick it up where you left off.

Download for Mac Read before installing

macOS 15 or later · Apple silicon · Needs OpenClaw installed · Open source (MIT) · iPhone app coming to the App Store

End-to-end encrypted

Messages are sealed on your devices. The relay passes ciphertext it has no key for.

Approvals on the lock screen

When an agent needs a yes or no, answer from the notification without opening the app.

No Yorozu account

Pair with one QR code. Your agents, models and credentials stay on your Mac.

Before you install

Using Yorozu could get your Claude or ChatGPT/Codex account suspended or banned. Yorozu drives Claude Code and Codex on your Mac with the account you signed in with. Anthropic and OpenAI decide what use of their subscriptions is allowed, change those rules without notice, and have suspended accounts used with third-party agent tools. If you want the lowest risk, use an API key rather than a subscription login.

AI agents take real actions on your Mac: they run commands, edit and delete files, and can send messages. Review what you approve. See the Terms of Use.
