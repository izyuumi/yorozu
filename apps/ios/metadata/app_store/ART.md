# App Store art

Style: the website's paper (#F1ECE4), slate (#4C5760) and vermilion (#BC2D1C), New York serif headlines with the
second line in vermilion italic, and the app icon's loop mark as a faint background motif. Every phone shows real UI
cropped from the 0.10.0 App Store screenshots, framed in the website's iPhone bezel.

- Header (3840x1646): one idea, "Your agent, anywhere.", matching the subtitle. The text sits inside Apple's template
  art-safe area (1646x661 at 1097,493) and the phones are on the wings, so cropping on an iPhone held upright only loses
  decoration. `en-US/creative/header-textless.png` carries the brand mark instead of text, for every other language.
- Search results (3840x2560): "Chat with your own agent. A native app for OpenClaw.", next to real chat UI, inside the
  2168x1030 safe area. The English one also serves the languages without their own.
- Screenshots 1-3 (6.9"): 1 "Your agent, in your pocket." (a trip-planning chat; privacy in the subline), 2 "Turn a
  chat into a routine." (the chat that creates the Morning check-in), 3 "Make it yours." (the 8 accent colors of 0.10).

en-US and ja have their own; the other seven languages use the textless header and the English search art.
