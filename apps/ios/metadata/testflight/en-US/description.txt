Yorozu is a native chat app for your own OpenClaw Gateway. Pair once with a link from your Gateway, then chat with your agent, follow its replies and tool calls, manage conversations and automations, and get notified when it answers, over an end-to-end encrypted relay. Requires your own OpenClaw Gateway with the Yorozu plugin. No Yorozu account. Enter YOROZU-DEMO on the pairing screen to try a demo.
