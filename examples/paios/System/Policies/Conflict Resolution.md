# Conflict Resolution Policy

When records conflict:

1. Preserve both claims and their evidence.
2. Check whether they differ by time, context, scope, entity identity, source reliability, or meaning.
3. Prefer an explicit current user statement for present intent.
4. Model contextual rules when supported, such as “prefers self-hosting when operational burden is reasonable.”
5. Mark unresolved conflicts `disputed` rather than selecting a convenient winner.
6. Ask the user only when the unresolved distinction materially affects an important decision or action.
7. Record the resolution and why it was adopted; retain the superseded states in the timeline.

Contradictions in behavior are evidence about context, not proof that the user is inconsistent. A purchase may reflect availability, deadline, or price rather than a durable brand preference.
