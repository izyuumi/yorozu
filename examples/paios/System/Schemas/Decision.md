## Required body sections

- `## Problem`
- `## Constraints and assumptions`
- `## Options considered`
- `## Tradeoffs`
- `## Decision`
- `## Why this option won`
- `## Confidence and unknowns`
- `## Consequences and review trigger`
- `## Evidence`

Store a concise, user-useful rationale summary. Do not claim hidden chain-of-thought or token-by-token reasoning.
