# Mental Model Schema

Mental models are predictive, probabilistic records about recurring people, organizations, or the user.

```yaml
id: mental-model-01J...
type: mental_model
schema_version: 1
status: active
title: Communication model — Example Person
subject_id: entity-...
model_domain: communication
confidence: 0.67
epistemic_status: inferred
last_verified: 2026-07-31
review_after: 2026-10-31
source_refs: []
created: 2026-07-31T12:00:00+00:00
updated: 2026-07-31T12:00:00+00:00
```

## Body sections

- `## Observed patterns`
- `## Likely preferences or concerns`
- `## Predictions`
- `## Supporting and contrary evidence`
- `## Confidence by claim`
- `## Boundaries and unknowns`

Do not store exact addresses, secrets, unsupported sensitive traits, or speculation as fact. Use these models to adapt respectfully, not manipulate.
