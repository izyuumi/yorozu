# Example — research a product before buying

## Trigger and scope

The owner asks which of a few products to buy.

## Preconditions

Budget and must-have features are known, or asked for once.

## Procedure

1. Read the owner's matching preferences in `Knowledge/Preferences/`.
2. Compare current prices and reviews from sources opened in this task.
3. Recommend one option with the evidence.

## Commit point and authorization check

Placing the order is the commit point. It needs a current explicit instruction or an active authorization that matches every material dimension.

## Verification

Order confirmation page or email, recorded without payment details.
