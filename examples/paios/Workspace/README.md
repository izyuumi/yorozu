# Workspace

Persistent working documents that may span sessions. Their contents are not automatically true or adopted.

- `Active/`: current working documents.
- `Investigations/`: open questions and diagnostic work.
- `Research/`: source-backed research notes.
- `Drafts/`: messages, documents, and uncommitted proposals.
- `Plans/`: plans not yet adopted as projects or actions.
- `Scratch/`: temporary material to consolidate or archive.
